# Code of Conduct

EON follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

**Do:** be kind, assume good intent, give and accept feedback gracefully, and credit other people's work.

**Don't:** harass or insult anyone, make personal or political attacks, publish someone's private information,
or sexualize people or discussion.

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere else you represent the project.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable, please tell the maintainer privately through
[this private form](https://github.com/samim-reza/EON/security/advisories/new). Say that it's a conduct report.
Reports are kept confidential.

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, and may warn or ban anyone who breaks these rules.
